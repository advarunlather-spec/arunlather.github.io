About

Arun Lather, B.Com., LL.B. (Hons.) is an Advocate practising before the Punjab & Haryana High Court and District & Sessions Courts, Jind.

The practice is focused on professional legal consultation, litigation support, legal drafting and representation, with an emphasis on preparation, confidentiality and client communication.
